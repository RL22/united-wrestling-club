## Detailed Image Evaluations

### Still 11: `still_11_packed_room_drilling.jpg`
- **Visual Description:** Wide shot of adult and youth athletes jogging in a circular warm-up drill across the red and grey mats. Large UWC wall mural in background right.
- **Facial & Eye Clarity:** Poor. Foreground athlete on left is seen from the rear; foreground runner in bottom center has back of head directly facing lens; middle athletes have motion-softened faces.
- **Sharpness & Motion Blur:** **4/10**. Significant shutter motion blur on limbs, feet, and faces. Paused frame looks muddy rather than intentional.
- **Composition & Negative Space:** Cluttered and bisected. A thick red architectural support column runs vertically down the left third (`x ≈ 34%`), cutting the composition in half.
- **Lighting & Contrast:** Flat warehouse fluorescent lighting with harsh white specular reflections on the red vinyl mat.
- **Excludes Check:** Foreground back-of-head truncation, vertical support beam dividing the room, Monster Energy bag on far left.
- **Verdict:** **REJECT**.
- **Best Placement:** None. Does not meet commercial brand standards for static web use.
