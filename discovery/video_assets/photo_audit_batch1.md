## 2. In-Depth Technical & Directorial Audits

### Still 01: `still_01_olympic_flag.jpg`
- **Visual Description:** Crisp wall banner featuring the official USA Wrestling red, white, and blue logo with "USA WRESTLING" bold typography. Left shows black JBL wall speaker; right shows LED match clock display ("25:21:").
- **Facial & Eye Clarity:** N/A (Branded facility object).
- **Sharpness & Motion Blur (8.0/10):** Sharp vector-level edges on the banner textile and vinyl lettering; LED matrix clearly defined. Static frame, zero blur.
- **Composition & Negative Space:** The white wall beneath the banner provides ample negative space, but the hardware framing (speaker mount and clock edge) gives it an organic gym snapshot vibe rather than a standalone graphic.
- **Lighting & Contrast:** High contrast against the off-white dry wall.
- **Excludes Check:** Speaker bracket and edge of framed poster on far left. No distracting people.
- **Art Direction Verdict:** `ACCEPT (Supporting/Card)`
- **Art Direction Notes & Web Placement:** Do not use as a primary hero. Crop tightly to 16:9 or 1:1 focusing strictly on the banner and clock as an authentic "Certified USA Wrestling Regional Training Center" proof asset inside an "Affiliations & Sanctions" strip, or complement SVG logos with this genuine facility snapshot.

### Still 02: `still_02_youth_parent_lounge.jpg`
- **Visual Description:** Youth wrestler sprawling on red mats in foreground; waiting youth wrestlers lined up against background wall; red cinema parent seating on right.
- **Facial & Eye Clarity:** Unfavorable. Foreground athlete's face is angled downwards toward the mat, masked in shadow with motion blur across the chin and hands. Background kids appear idle, slouched, or looking sideways.
- **Sharpness & Motion Blur (5.0/10):** Moderate-to-heavy motion blur on the primary moving subject; loss of micro-contrast in facial features.
- **Composition & Negative Space:** Visually fragmented. Left edge has a partial cutoff of another wrestler in white sweatpants; right side is cluttered with theater seating and loose gear.
- **Excludes Check:** Awkward body cutoff on left; disorganized background line.
- **Art Direction Verdict:** `REJECT`
- **Art Direction Notes:** Lacks professional dynamic tension. Represents typical candid gym video rather than an aspirational marketing asset. Discard.

### Still 03: `still_03_varsity_stance_focus.jpg`
- **Visual Description:** Young varsity-tier wrestler locked into a ready stance with hands up, elbows tucked, knees bent, framed against a grey padded wall and the iconic red-and-black Reebok wall mat.
- **Facial & Eye Clarity:** Exceptional (9/10). Eyes are wide, intensely focused, and fixed on an imaginary opponent. Determined jawline, authentic athletic expression without comical grimacing.
- **Sharpness & Motion Blur (8.5/10):** Very crisp focus on eyes, hair, face, and chest. Hands show subtle natural motion blur that conveys ready reflexes without softening the anatomy.
- **Composition & Negative Space:** Athlete sits nicely in the right two-thirds. Red mat anchors the bottom; neutral grey wall creates an ideal clean backdrop.
- **Excludes Check:** Partial wrestler visible on far left in camo singlet.
- **Art Direction Verdict:** `ACCEPT (Supporting/Card)`
- **Art Direction Notes & Web Placement:** Crop the left 20% to eliminate the secondary wrestler. This isolates the athlete into a powerhouse 4:5 vertical portrait or 16:9 card for `/classes/varsity-prep` or `/classes/youth-wrestling` ("Discipline, Focus & Mental Toughness").

### Color Grading & Post-Processing Directives for Approved Stills:
1. **White Balance Uniformity:** Video frames vary slightly between warm red mat bounce and cool overhead skylight. Warm up `still_06` by +200K to match skin tones, and pull down cyan tint in `still_10`.
2. **Contrast & Clarity:** Apply gentle S-curve (+8 contrast) to `still_06` and `still_07` to enhance singlet textile details without blowing out specular highlights on sweating skin.
3. **Typography Overlay Prep:** For `still_06` (Hero candidate), apply a subtle linear gradient overlay from the right edge (0% opacity at 50% width to 45% dark charcoal `#111827` at right boundary) to ensure white headline text passes WCAG AA contrast standards.
