COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child begin training? +
Children can start as early as age 5 in our Youth Wrestling Academy. At this age, curriculum focuses on agility, tumbling, balance, basic stances, and listening discipline in an engaging, supportive environment.
What gear does my child need for their 3-day trial? +
For the 3-day trial pass, your child just needs comfortable athletic shorts (without zippers or pockets) and a t-shirt. Wrestling shoes are recommended but socks are acceptable for their first trial days. We provide loaner headgear if needed. Arrive 15 minutes early for check-in and gear fitting, and text (510) 303-4869 to confirm your first session.
Are there contracts or cancellation fees? +
No. All memberships are flat $139.99 month-to-month. There are no initiation fees, no belt exam fees, and you may cancel or pause your membership anytime with 7 days advance notice before your billing cycle.
Where is the gym located and which cities do you serve? +
United Wrestling Club is located at 30139 Industrial Pkwy SW Ste C, Hayward, CA 94544, easily accessible via I-880 and Highway 92. We serve families and competitors from Hayward, Union City, Fremont, Newark, Castro Valley, and San Leandro.
