# Live Site Content: Legacy / Unlinked Class Routes

> These 6 routes exist in the Next.js build manifest (`_buildManifest.js`) using an older template with `uwc_place.webp` hero banners and bullet lists, but are unlinked from the main navigation dropdown. They should be **301 redirected** in the rebuild.

## 1. `/classes/fundamental-boxing` (Duplicate of `/classes/boxing-fundamentals`)
- **Title:** `Fundamental Boxing`
- **Copy:** Boxing Fundamentals Class covering topics such as jumping rope, stance, movement, offensive/defensive technique through group exercises, coach guided break downs, 1-1 partner drilling, and open practice sessions. This class is open for anyone starting their first day of boxing to intermediate boxers that are looking to sharpen their tools in the ring.
- **Bullets:** `Fundamentals`, `Basic Combos`, `Basic Stance`, `Basic Defence`, `Lateral Movement`

## 2. `/classes/jiu-jitsu` (Superseded by `/classes/gi-jiu-jitsu` & `/classes/nogi-jiu-jitsu`)
- **Title:** `Jiu Jitsu`
- **Copy:** Jiu Jitsu classes are a fun and engaging class that’s great for both beginners and more experienced competitors alike. UWC Jiu Jitsu classes offer high level Brazilian Jiu Jitsu techniques that will enhance your submission grappling game to the next level.
- **Bullets:** `Basic Chokes`, `Basic Choke Defense`, `Side Control`, `Full Mount`, `Full Guard`

## 3. `/classes/intensity-training`
- **Title:** `Intensity Training`
- **Copy:** HIIT class is geared to prepare individuals for endurance. This heart pounding class can be used for weight loss, competition preparation, group exercise, or simply just to get your blood flow going. For all ages and levels. 12 Rounds of 3 minutes.
- **Bullets:** `Jump Rope`, `High Intensity Movements`, `Battle Ropes`, `Punching bag`, `Kicking Bag`, `Stationary Bike`, `Work Out Stations`, `Elliptical`

## 4. `/classes/strength-and-conditioning`
- **Title:** `Strength & Conditioning`
- **Copy:** Strength & Conditioning class focuses on plyometric exercises and metabolic conditioning used in wrestling to develop core strength maturity and lean muscle. For all levels and ages, this class will cover:
- **Bullets:** `Resistance Training`, `KettleBell Training`, `Battle Rope Training`, `Agility Ladder`, `Compound Movements`, `Weight Training`

## 5. `/classes/wrestling-technique`
- **Title:** `Wrestling Technique`
- **Copy:** Wrestling Technique class is offered to kids and adults of all levels who want to learn the fundamentals of each wrestling technique and the practical methods on how to execute on such techniques.
- **Bullets:** `Warm up`, `Fundamental Wrestling Movement`, `Offensive Wrestling Technique`, `Defensive Wrestling Technique`, `Chain Wrestling`, `Wrestling Throws`

## 6. `/classes/rest-and-recovery`
- **Title:** `Rest & Recovery`
- **Copy:** Rest & Recovery class aims to help the body recover, repair, and decompress stiffness and sore muscles. Recovery is a vital part of training and avoiding injury. For all ages and levels.
- **Bullets:** `Low Intensity Movements`, `Light Cardio`, `Stretching`, `Breathing`, `Muscle Release`, `Flexibility Exercises`, `Mobility Exercises`
