- **H2:** `Our Legacy`
- **Image:** `/founder.jpg` (`alt="Karim Zemaryalai"`)
- **Body Copy:**
  Founded by **Karim Zemaryalai**, a distinguished figure in the wrestling world, United Wrestling Club stands as a testament to the pursuit of excellence in the sport. Karim brings with him a wealth of experience and accolades including a prestigious **silver medal** in the Ali Aliyev tournament in Makhachkala, Dagestan and qualified for the **1980 Moscow Olympics**. His journey as an athlete and a coach has changed the ethos of United Wrestling Club infusing it with a commitment to relentless training, strategic skill development, and unwavering determination. United Wrestling Club proudly continues its legacy under the stewardship of Karim's son, Naweed Zemaryalai who has been appointed as the Head Wrestling Coach building upon his father's esteemed achievements and teachings.
