## 1. The Core Problem & Guiding Principle

### Why Most Martial Arts Guest Programs Fail Financially
1. **Flat Hourly Payroll Failure:** If the gym pays instructors $35–$50/hr regardless of turnout, the gym loses money when attendance is low.
2. **Mat Rental Failure:** If the gym charges instructors $75/hr mat rent, instructors carry all marketing risk; when promotion falters, the instructors leave.
3. **One-Off Workshop Dead-End:** A single Saturday seminar generates quick cash ($500), but creates zero recurring monthly revenue for the club.

### The Guiding Financial Principle: "Zero Risk, Aligned Incentives, Lifetime Value"
* **UWC provides:** Premium Olympic facility, mat insurance, registration software (`WrestlingIQ`), SEO organic traffic, and email/SMS broadcast.
* **Instructors provide:** Elite curriculum, high-authority personal brands, social reach, and live coaching execution.
* **Compensation structure:** Performance-based percentage splits on the front end + **recurring bounty/rollover incentives** on back-end membership conversions.

## 3. Tier-by-Tier Financial Breakdown & Splits

### Tier 1: Front-End Saturday Workshop (*"The 2.5-Hour Real-World Defense Intensive"*)
* **Ticket Price:** `$49` per attendee (Early bird `$39` / At-door `$59`).
* **Capacity Cap:** 20 attendees per workshop (maintains 7:1 coach-to-student ratio across Louisa, Neha's Sister, Ja Shia).
* **Schedule Slot:** Saturday 12:30 PM – 3:00 PM (immediately following Saturday 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM Open Mat; utilizes dead mat space).
* **Gross Revenue per Workshop (at 20 attendees):** `$980.00` (net ~$950 after Stripe/WrestlingIQ processing).
* **Revenue Split (60% Instructors / 40% UWC):**
  * **Instructor Pool (60% = $570):** Split equally among the 3 co-instructors = **`$190` per instructor** for 2.5 hours of work (**`$76/hour` effective rate**).

### Tier 4: The Backend LTV Multiplier (*The 100% Tuition Rollover*)

The real wealth for UWC is not the seminar ticket; it is the **lifetime subscription value (LTV)** of converting workshop attendees into recurring members.

#### The Offer Mechanic:
> *"If you join UWC's monthly Adult Wrestle-Jitsu, Boxing, or Open Mat membership ($139.99/mo) within 7 days of completing the seminar or cohort, 100% of your registration fee ($49 or $199) is applied as a tuition credit across your first 2–3 months."*

#### Conversion Unit Economics:
* **Cohort Attendance:** 16 women.
* **Conservative Conversion Rate (30%):** 5 women enroll in ongoing training (`$139.99/mo`).
* **Average Student Retention:** 12 months.
* **New Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) for UWC:**  
  $$\text{5 students} \times \$139.99/\text{mo} \times 12\text{ months} = \mathbf{\$8,399.40/\text{year}}$$
* **Instructor Ongoing Incentive ("Coach Bounty"):**
  * UWC pays a **`$50.00` one-time affiliate bounty** to the instructors for every cohort graduate who signs an active monthly membership.
  * 5 conversions = **`$250.00` bonus** split among Louisa, Neha's Sister, and Ja Shia, aligning their coaching toward long-term retention.
* **Cross-Sell Secondary Pipeline:**
  * 20% of adult women attendees are mothers who subsequently enroll their children in UWC's **Pre-K–5th Kids Wrestling (`$89.99–$139.99/mo`)** or **Competition Team (`$150/mo`)**, generating another **`$2,500–$5,000/yr`** in household LTV.
