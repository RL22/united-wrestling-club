## 2. Commercial & Funnel Bottlenecks Logged

1. **WrestlingIQ Redirect Drop-Off (40%–60% Abandonment):**  
   Every "Register" CTA abruptly ejects prospective families to `wrestlingiq.com`. Forcing registration and card entry before answering basic parent questions causes severe traffic leakage.
2. **Fragmented & Conflicting Front-End Trial Messaging:**  
   The homepage advertises "$15 for 3-Day Trial", `/pricing` advertises "$15 Drop In", and `/faqs` states "Free trial sessions." Cold prospects encounter immediate cognitive friction.
3. **Zero Automated Lead Capture & High-Friction Onboarding:**  
   No mechanism captures visitor name/phone before checkout. Evening inbound calls (5:00 PM – 9:00 PM) go to voicemail while coaches are on the mat, with no instant SMS reply or arrival guide.
4. **Absence of Seasonal Retention Locks:**  
   Pure month-to-month billing leaves UWC completely exposed to November and May enrollment lulls, with no advance commitment mechanisms to stabilize cash flow.

## 3. Approved Commercial Fixes & Core Pricing Model

*Per Approved Architecture & ADR-20261102:*

1. **Low-Risk Acquisition Entry — $15 3-Day Mat Pass:**
   - 3 consecutive practices, equipment fitting, and 1-on-1 technique assessment with Coach Naweed.
   - Captured via native 1-click modal on `/register` without off-site redirect.
   - Local East Bay Resident Option: Free 2-Class Assessment Pass (anchored against $30 drop-in value).
2. **Transparent Core Public Pricing — $139.99 / Month:**
   - Unlimited month-to-month access across wrestling and grappling.
   - Zero initiation fees, zero belt exam fees, and zero cancellation penalties (7 days advance notice).
   - Kids 2-Day Foundation maintained at `$89.99/mo`; Competition Team at `$150.00/mo`.
   - 15% Sibling / Household discount for secondary family members.
3. **Flexibility Option — $15 Single Mat Drop-In:**
   - Retained for traveling athletes and Saturday Regional Open Mat (`Session #2664`).
4. **Rejection of Mandatory 12-Month Contracts:**
   - UWC explicitly rejects predatory lock-in contracts, turning transparent month-to-month billing into an anti-positioning weapon against corporate karate and BJJ gyms charging $225–$275/mo.

## 4. Strategic Enhancements & High-Margin Growth Levers

### Lever A: Seasonal Paid-In-Full (PIF) Loyalty Promotions
- **Timing:** Launched 30–45 days ahead of November and May historical dips.
- **Audience:** Exclusive loyalty incentive for active, committed members (no public discounting).
- **Offer Structure:** "Pay for 10 months upfront, get 12 months" (or 6-Month Block: Pay 5, get 6) + free custom UWC compression rashguard.
- **Financial Impact:** Locks in retention across slow months, insulates against churn, and injects **$15,000–$25,000 upfront cash** per promotional cycle.

### Lever B: Women's Self-Defense Program & Corporate Clinics
- **Instructors:** Louisa (Boxing/MMA & entrepreneur), Neha's Sister (police officer & UWC alumna/champion), Ja Shia (Oakland hand-to-hand combat).
- **Mat Utilization:** Monetizes dead weekend mat hours (Saturdays 12:30–3:00 PM, Sundays 10:30 AM–12:00 PM).
- **Revenue Engine:**
  - *Tier 1 (Saturday Workshop, $49/ticket, 20 cap):* `$980 gross` ($380 net to UWC / $570 to instructors, 60/40 split).
  - *Tier 2 (6-Week Cohort, $199/student, 16 cap):* `$3,184 gross` ($1,236 net to UWC / $1,854 to instructors).
  - *Tier 3 (Corporate B2B Safety Clinics, $1,000–$1,500/clinic):* 70/30 split with Kaiser Hayward, local realtors, and school districts.
  - *Tier 4 (100% Tuition Rollover to Monthly Membership):* 5 conversions/cohort = **$8,399/year in new ARR** per cohort with a $50 instructor bounty pool.
- **Cohort Potential:** **$10,000–$15,000** in blended annual value per cohort cycle.

### Lever C: UWC Community Mixer & Free Open Mat Day
- High-energy Saturday community open house at `30139 Industrial Pkwy SW Ste C`.
- Free youth and adult introductory clinics, VIP guest appearances, and default opt-out media release.
- Yields immediate trial enrollments while capturing 12 months of high-resolution marketing assets and video testimonials.
