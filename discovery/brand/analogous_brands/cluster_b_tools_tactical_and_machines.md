## TL;DR

- Analyzes four industrial and tactical brands (Milwaukee Tool, G-Shock / Casio, Toyota TRD / Land Cruiser, Sinn / Tudor) for the UWC 2027 visual and UI rebuild.
- Each brand's design DNA (type, palette, photography, components) is mapped to a UWC application, such as modular spec cards and a "300,000-mile chassis" hero campaign.
- Part 5 compares the four brands in one matrix and sets a combined UWC 2027 spec with a cast-iron black background and heritage crimson and TRD orange accents.
