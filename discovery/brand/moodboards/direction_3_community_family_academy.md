# Brand Direction 3: Community Family Academy & Civic Empowerment

> Sharded on 2026-11-08. The full text is in [`direction_3_community_family_academy/`](direction_3_community_family_academy/). Edit the parts, not this index.

## TL;DR

- Direction 3, "Community Family Academy & Civic Empowerment," moves the brand from dark combat styling to warm parchment and Pacific Slate with Plus Jakarta Sans and softer radii.
- Its target audience is East Bay families, women seeking realistic self-defense, adult beginners, and civic partners.
- Part 4 defines four color-coded family track cards and a one-click trial pass modal with a media opt-out option.
- Part 5 lists civic integrations: Hayward DSAL youth clinic badges, school feeder programs, and healthcare and first-responder safety seminars.
