# Brand Direction 1: The Olympic Heritage & Dagestan Gold

> Sharded on 2026-11-08. The full text is in [`direction_1_olympic_heritage/`](direction_1_olympic_heritage/). Edit the parts, not this index.

## TL;DR

- Direction 1, "The Olympic Heritage & Dagestan Gold," builds the brand on crimson, gold, and obsidian with a Cinzel, Oswald, and Montserrat type system.
- The narrative centers on founder Karim Zemaryalai's 1980 Moscow Olympic qualification and Ali Aliyev silver medal, plus clinic ties to Magomed Ramazanov.
- Part 2 sets a type hierarchy table and a button suite with gold primary, heritage secondary, and ghost tertiary styles.
- Part 3 covers a desktop hero wireframe, a lineage credential grid, and photography rules that reject fluorescent gym light and posed smiles.
