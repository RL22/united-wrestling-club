# United Wrestling Club (UWC) — Brand Direction 2: "Modern Combat Athleticism & East Bay Grit"

> Sharded on 2026-11-08. The full text is in [`direction_2_modern_combat_athletic/`](direction_2_modern_combat_athletic/). Edit the parts, not this index.

## TL;DR

- Direction 2, "Modern Combat Athleticism & East Bay Grit," pairs a carbon-black canvas with high-voltage crimson and cyan telemetry accents.
- Typography uses Space Grotesk and Montserrat for headings, with JetBrains Mono for HUD labels and key stats.
- Part 2 proposes a filterable schedule grid of machined cards to fix the live site's duplicate 7:30 and 8:00 PM rows and missing category filters.
- Part 3 specifies photography (1/1000s shutter, low-key rim light), assigns live-vault assets to page sections, and includes a desktop hero wireframe.
