## 1. Practice Overview & Executive Summary

**United Wrestling Club (UWC)** is an elite combat sports and wrestling academy located at **30139 Industrial Pkwy SW in Hayward, California**. 

Founded by legendary international competitor **Karim Zemaryalai**—a 1980 Moscow Olympics qualifier and bronze medalist at the prestigious Ali Aliyev Tournament in Dagestan—the academy is currently spearheaded by Head Wrestling Coach **Naweed Zemaryalai** (former Chabot College standout, SF State Kinesiology alumnus, and Chabot College coach). 

UWC bridges a critical gap in Bay Area athletics by offering high-pedigree Olympic Folkstyle, Freestyle, and Greco-Roman wrestling alongside Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (Gi and No-Gi "Wrestle-Jitsu"), Combat Sambo (coached by a Kyrgyz master), and boxing fundamentals. The club caters to youth athletes (Pre-K through 12th grade), scholastic competitors preparing for CIF/NCAA competition, and adults ranging from beginner hobbyists to competitive MMA fighters.

## 4. Training Programs & Core Disciplines

### A. Youth & Scholastic Wrestling
* **Kids Wrestling (Pre-K to 5th Grade / Ages 4–11):** Stance, motion, basic takedowns, pinning combinations, spatial discipline, and motor skills.
* **Middle & High School Wrestling (Grades 6–12 / Ages 12–18):** Advanced Folkstyle chaining, hand-fighting, mat returns, bottom escapes, and high-pace live simulation.
* **UWC Competition Team (Pre-K – 12th Grade):** Travel team competing in SCYWAY, USA Wrestling, and CIF scholastic circuits across California.

### B. Adult Wrestling & Mixed Grappling
* **Adult Wrestling (Beginners, College, MMA):** High-output wrestling practice designed for adults, former competitors, and MMA/BJJ fighters seeking takedown mastery.
* **Gi Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu:** Traditional gi submission grappling emphasizing guard retention, sweeps, and positional control.
* **No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu ("Wrestle-Jitsu"):** Hybrid grappling integrating collegiate wrestling top pressure, takedowns, and front headlocks with submission finishing.
* **Combat Sambo:** Upper-body jacket throws, leg sweeps, and dynamic leg locks.
* **Boxing Fundamentals:** Stance, footwork, jab/cross combinations, slip lines, and distance management.
* **HIIT / Intensity Conditioning:** 12 rounds of 3-minute high-intensity metabolic conditioning.
* **Open Mat (Saturdays 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM):** Multi-discipline live sparring and drilling.
