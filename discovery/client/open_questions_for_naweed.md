# Open questions for Naweed

## TL;DR
- Six facts conflict across our sources. None of them is on the live site yet.
- Answering these unblocks copy, schema, and the internal planning numbers.
- Reply by number. "Don't publish" is a valid answer.

## 1. Coach-to-athlete ratio
Our facility ADR and knowledge base say **1:6**. The analogous-brand spec sheet says **1:8**.
Is 1:6 accurate for every session, or only some? Can we quote it publicly?

## 2. Founding year
The brand ethos badges say **Est. 1980**. The Direction 2 wireframe says **EST. 2021**.
Which year should the site show? Or should we not show one?

## 3. Sibling discount
The commercial ADR proposes a **15% additional-household discount**. The knowledge base says a sibling discount is **not on the public price list** and must not be quoted.
Do you offer it? Should it be published?

## 4. Steve Siroy's Chabot tenure

## 5. Trial show-up rate
The commercial synthesis uses about **45%** of trials showing up. The SMS playbook uses about **55%**.
What is your actual figure from trial attendance? This is for planning only and is not published.

## 6. Women's self-defense seminar price
