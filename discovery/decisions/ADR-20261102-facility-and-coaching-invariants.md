# ADR-20261102-facility-and-coaching-invariants: Physical Facility Footprint & Coaching Pedigree Invariants

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Status:** Approved
- **Scope:** Commercial / Technical / Content

## Context & Problem
Earlier drafts and prototype copy mistakenly referenced "5,000 SQ FT" for the mat space footprint in several ribbons, meta tags, and pricing descriptions. In addition, the Olympic heritage of Karim Zemaryalai was being repeated multiple times in close proximity, crowding out other verified coaching staff credentials and operational safety details.

## Approved Solution
1. **Physical Facility Invariants:**
   - **Mat Footprint:** Exactly **3,000 sq ft** of seamless Dollamur competition mats. All 8 legacy occurrences of "5,000 sq ft" were purged and corrected across meta tags, OpenGraph descriptions, sanction ribbons, schedule cards, pricing perks, and FAQ answers.
   - **Sanitization Protocol:** Mopped twice daily (`2x/day`) before and after training blocks using hospital-grade antimicrobial solutions (effective against MRSA, ringworm, and staph).
   - **Coach-to-Athlete Ratio:** Dedicated **1:6 ratio** ensuring close individual correction and safe partner matching.
   - **Facility Address:** 30139 Industrial Pkwy SW Ste C, Hayward, CA 94544 (USA Wrestling RTC #513).
2. **Coaching Lineage Invariants:**
   - **Founder Karim Zemaryalai:** 1980 Moscow Olympic qualifier and Ali Aliyev International Tournament medalist in Dagestan. Lineage referenced at most once prominently in the Hero and detailed in the Coaching Dossier.
   - **Head Coach Naweed Zemaryalai:** B.S. in Kinesiology (San Francisco State University), collegiate wrestling coach at Chabot College, corners undefeated UFC bantamweight contender Farid Basharat.
   - **Secondary Bench (4 Verified Coaches):**
     1. Rashad Rahimi: Co-Founder, Asian Games Silver Medalist, 1980 Olympic Qualifier.
     2. Sandeep Singh Gosal: 3x California Greco-Roman State Champion, USA Wrestling Silver Certified Coach.
     3. Steve Siroy: 40-year Head Coach at Chabot College, California Wrestling Hall of Fame inductee.
     4. Farid Siddiq: Striking & Boxing Director, collegiate athlete, combat footwork coach.

## Alternatives Rejected
1. **Retaining "5,000 sq ft" as Marketing Hyperbole:** Rejected because factual inflation damages credibility with local parents and wrestling coaches who inspect the physical room.
2. **Repeating Olympic Mentions in Every Section:** Rejected because over-repetition dilutes impact and sounds desperate; reserving Olympic mentions for high-value authority anchors strengthens the moat.
