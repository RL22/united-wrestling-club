# ADR-20261102-commercial-pricing-and-pif-promotions: Commercial Pricing Strategy & Seasonal Paid-In-Full (PIF) Promotions

- **Date:** 2026-11-02
- **Status:** Approved
- **Scope:** Commercial / Policy / Retention

## Context & Problem
Combat sports academies often debate whether to lock members into 12-month legal contracts or operate strictly month-to-month. In youth sports and local wrestling, mandatory long-term contracts create acute signup friction for hesitant parents, generate high churn and negative reviews when families move or sports seasons change, and conflict with UWC's core value of integrity. However, pure month-to-month billing exposes the club to seasonal enrollment dips (notably November/December holiday travel and May/June pre-summer transition).

## Approved Solution
1. **Core Public Membership Model:**
   - **Low-Risk Acquisition:** **\$15 3-Day Mat Pass** (3 consecutive practices, equipment fitting, and 1-on-1 technique assessment with Coach Naweed).
   - **Unlimited Membership:** **\$139.99/month** month-to-month with zero initiation fees, zero belt exam fees, and zero cancellation penalties (7 days advance notice).
   - **Flexibility Option:** **\$15 Single Mat Drop-In** (1 training session or Saturday Bay Area Open Mat).
   - **Family Discount:** 15% discount for second sibling or family member.
2. **Rejection of Mandatory 12-Month Contracts:**
   - UWC explicitly rejects predatory 12-month lock-in agreements as an anti-positioning weapon against franchise fitness gyms and McDojos.
3. **Strategic Paid-In-Full (PIF) Loyalty Promotions:**
   - Run targeted PIF promotions strictly as loyalty rewards ahead of historical slow seasons (30–45 days prior to November and May).
   - Offer existing, high-attendance members a discounted 6-month or 12-month upfront renewal (e.g., Pay for 10 months, receive 12 months, plus an exclusive UWC team compression rashguard).
   - Captures upfront working capital, locks in retention through slow months, and reinforces member goodwill without alienating incoming trial prospects.

## Alternatives Rejected
1. **Mandatory 12-Month Contracts:** Rejected because they suppress initial trial conversion by 40–60% and damage UWC's community trust.
2. **Public Discounting & Groupons:** Rejected because public price slashing degrades brand prestige, attracts non-committed churners, and insults loyal paying members.
