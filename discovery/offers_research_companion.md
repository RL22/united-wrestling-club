## 4. ICP Buyer Psychology & Objection-to-Bonus Mapping

### Avatar 1: East Bay Wrestling & Martial Arts Parents (Ages 4–14)
* **Surface Ask:** *"I want after-school wrestling or martial arts classes for my kid in Hayward/Union City/Fremont."*
* **Dream Outcome:** *"I want my child off screens, physically strong, un-bullyable, disciplined, and coached by serious role models without getting concussions."*
* **Hidden Fears (Why They Bounce Today):**
  1. *"Is wrestling too rough for a 6-year-old beginner?"* → Solved by **Weight & Age-Matched Pairing Protocol** + **14-Day Mat-Fit Guarantee**.
  2. *"Do I have to buy wrestling shoes, headgear, and a singlet before I know if they like it?"* → Solved by **No-Gear First 30 Days Bonus**.
  3. *"I clicked Register Now and it asked for my credit card on WrestlingIQ before I even talked to a coach."* → Solved by **The Olympic Roots 2-Class Assessment Pass** (native SMS modal, no card required).

### Avatar 2: High School Scholastic Wrestlers & Parents (Grades 6–12 / MVAL & WACC)
* **Surface Ask:** *"Where can I wrestle off-season near Fremont, Union City, or Hayward?"*
* **Dream Outcome:** *"I want to dominate my weight class at NCS and qualify for the CIF State Tournament / wrestle in college."*
* **Hidden Fears (Why They Bounce Today):**
  1. *"Will this club actually teach high-level technique or is it just a babysitting rec club?"* → Solved by highlighting **Karim Zemaryalai (1980 Olympian / Ali Aliyev Dagestan Medalist)**, **Naweed Zemaryalai (Chabot College Coach)**, and **Sandeep Singh Gosal (James Logan / Fresno State)**.
  2. *"Are there enough tough partners in my weight class?"* → Solved by **Saturday Multi-Discipline Open Mat Access** + **East Bay Varsity & CIF Pipeline** positioning.

### Avatar 3: Adult Grapplers, BJJ Players & MMA Fighters (Ages 18–40)
* **Surface Ask:** *"Adult wrestling classes Bay Area"* or *"No-Gi takedown classes near me."*
* **Dream Outcome:** *"I want to stop pulling guard in BJJ tournaments, dictate where every fight takes place, and get in elite grappling shape without blowing out my knees."*
* **Hidden Fears (Why They Bounce Today):**
  1. *"Are adult wrestling classes even open to a 32-year-old who didn't wrestle in college?"* → Solved by **Progressive Live-Go Scaling** (explicitly welcoming adult beginners and BJJ cross-trainers).
  2. *"I already pay $220/mo at my BJJ gym; I can't afford another $220/mo membership."* → Solved by price-anchoring the **$100/mo (3-Day)** and **$139.99/mo (Unlimited All-Access)** month-to-month tiers as a high-ROI takedown & Sambo supplement or full replacement.
