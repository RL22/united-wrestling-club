## 2. Competitive Landscape Across the 3 Alameda County Hubs

### Hub A: South Alameda County / Tri-City (Fremont, Union City, Newark)
* **The Competition:** Dragon's Den Kajukenbo (Union City, 19 reviews), One World Jiu Jitsu (Newark, 29 reviews), Crossfit One World (Union City, 45 reviews), Koa Fitness (Newark, 31 reviews).
* **The Vulnerability:** None of these competitors offer authentic collegiate or Olympic Folkstyle/Freestyle wrestling. They are fitness gyms, traditional karate, or pure BJJ.
* **The Strategy:** Tri-City has large scholastic wrestling programs (James Logan HS in Union City, Irvington/Mission San Jose/Washington HS in Fremont). Coach Sandeep Gosal is a legendary James Logan wrestling alumnus. UWC can monopolize scholastic off-season training for Tri-City.

### Hub B: Hayward & Castro Valley
* **The Competition:** Osorio Training Center (Hayward, 54 reviews), EDGE Training Center (Hayward, 35 reviews), Mindset Martial Arts (Hayward, 85 reviews).
* **The Vulnerability:** Mindset and Osorio focus heavily on general fitness, striking, and kids karate. UWC has 42 reviews at a flawless 5.0★ rating with authentic Olympic pedigree.
* **The Strategy:** Add secondary Google Business Profile categories (*Wrestling School*, *Martial Arts School*, *Sports Club*) and target parent queries for *"Youth wrestling Hayward"* and *"Kids martial arts Castro Valley"*.

### Hub C: North Alameda County (Oakland)
* **The Competition:** ARMADA MMA (59 reviews), Four Elements Fitness (32 reviews), Pacific Ring Sports (52 reviews).
* **The Vulnerability:** High travel friction. Parents in Oakland will rarely drive 20 miles south to Hayward for weekday classes unless it's for weekend elite travel competition camps.
* **The Strategy:** Capture Oakland high school and adult wrestlers specifically for **Saturday Open Mat (10:00 AM – 12:00 PM)** and specialized weekend competition intensives.
