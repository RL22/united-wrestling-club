### 4.1 The "Next-Step Year-Round Academy" Conversion Funnel (The DSAL-to-UWC Pipeline)

#### The Problem with Free Rec Programs
When young athletes join DSAL at 360 Laurel Ave in Hayward, they experience the thrill of wrestling. But after 6 months, athletic kids and motivated parents hit the **rec ceiling**:
* Practices are only 2 days a week for 60 minutes.
* Mat time is divided between complete novices and rowdy first-timers.
* There is no weekend travel team, no SCYWAY tournament schedule, and no path to CIF state or college scholarships.

#### The Solution: The UWC Junior Olympian Bridge
1. **Coach-to-Coach Outreach:** Coach Naweed Zemaryalai reaches out to DSAL Hayward Head Coach Juana Juarez and BTSBA Executive Director Yero Washington.
2. **Cooperative Positioning:** UWC honors DSAL's mission to introduce wrestling to underserved youth at zero cost. UWC acts as the **"Year-Round Advanced Feeder Academy"**.
3. **The UWC Community Scholarship Fund:**
   * UWC commits to sponsoring **2 to 4 full need-based scholarships** annually for dedicated DSAL athletes who want to train 6 days a week on UWC's competition team.
   * For non-scholarship DSAL families, UWC offers an exclusive **"DSAL Graduate Transition Pass"**: First month at **$49.99** (regular $89.99–$139.99/mo) + **Free Competition Singlet / Rashguard**.
4. **On-Site Clinic Exchange:** Coach Karim Zemaryalai (Olympian) conducts a free 90-minute Dagestani wrestling clinic at 360 Laurel Ave once per semester.
