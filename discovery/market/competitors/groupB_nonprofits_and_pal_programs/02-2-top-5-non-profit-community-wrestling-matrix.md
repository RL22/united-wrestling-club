### Profile 1: Beat the Streets Bay Area / Alameda County DSAL (Hayward Hub)

* **Domains:** [btsbayarea.org](https://btsbayarea.org/) | [acdsal.org](https://acdsal.org/)
* **Physical Location:** **Alameda County DSAL Boxing Gym & Fitness Arena**, 360 Laurel Avenue, Hayward, CA 94541
* **Distance to UWC:** **4.8 miles (10–12 minute drive down Mission Blvd / Industrial Pkwy SW)**
* **OpenSEO Footprint:** `btsbayarea.org`: **74 monthly organic visits | 56 ranked keywords** (plus high domain authority from `acdsal.org` and `acgov.org`)

* **Tax & Governance Model:** 501(c)(3) Youth Development Non-Profit (founded 2019; affiliated with Beat the Streets National and BTS Los Angeles) in joint operating partnership with the Alameda County Sheriff’s Office Deputy Sheriffs' Activities League (DSAL).

#### A. Organizational Mission, Funding & Target Demographic
Beat the Streets Bay Area (BTSBA) was founded in 2019 to cultivate youth development in historically under-resourced communities through wrestling, academic support, and life mentorship. Led by Executive Director **Yero Washington** (who oversees both BTS Los Angeles and BTS Bay Area), Board Chairman **Brian Guerrero**, Associate Director of Operations **Olivia Olmedo** (UC Berkeley alumna), and Programs Assistant **Jaden Abas** (Stanford wrestling standout and NCAA All-American), BTSBA operates in Oakland, South San Francisco, and Hayward.

In Hayward, BTSBA partners directly with **Alameda County DSAL**—a non-profit founded by the Alameda County Sheriff's Office to substitute law enforcement intervention with sports, health, and vocational programming. The program targets children ages 5–18 residing in Hayward, Cherryland, Ashland, and San Lorenzo.

#### B. Current Program Offerings, Facilities & Weekly Schedule
* **Venue:** DSAL Boxing Gym and Fitness Arena, 360 Laurel Ave, Hayward, CA. (A city-owned community facility equipped with boxing rings, functional fitness turf, and a designated wrestling mat area).
* **Schedule:** Free co-ed youth practices held **Mondays and Fridays from 5:30 PM to 6:30 PM**.
* **Curriculum:** Entry-level introduction to wrestling rules, stance, motion, level changes, double-leg and single-leg takedowns, and half-nelson breakdowns.
* **Cost:** **$0.00 (100% Free)** funded through corporate grants, Alameda County safety initiatives, and philanthropic donations.

#### C. Coaching Staff & Institutional Ties
* **Head Coach (Hayward Site):** Coach **Juana Juarez**.
* **Supervising Leadership:** Yero Washington (BTSBA Executive Director) and Jaden Abas (Stanford wrestler).
* **Institutional Backlinks & Citations:** Backlinked from Alameda County government portals (`acgov.org`), Alameda County Sheriff's Office newsletters, Stanford University athletics outreach bulletins, and local school district family resource listings.

#### D. Algorithmic Root Cause: Why They Outrank Commercial Gyms in Alameda County
`btsbayarea.org` ranks **#6 Organic for *"wrestling clubs alameda county"*** because:
1. **Unrivaled Geographic Entity Association:** Search engines associate DSAL's physical headquarters (`360 Laurel Ave, Hayward`) with Alameda County youth programs through municipal records and local press.
2. **Topical Relevance for "Wrestling Club":** Unlike general fitness gyms (e.g., Smash Gyms or Osorio), every page on `btsbayarea.org` is saturated with core wrestling semantic entities: *"folkstyle wrestling", "freestyle", "wrestling mat", "youth wrestling", "takedowns", "high school wrestling"*.
3. **High Domain Authority Backlink Profile:** Citations from USA Wrestling, Stanford Athletics, Beat the Streets National (`btsnational.org`), and public school athletic directors give the domain an authoritative backlink baseline.

#### E. Operational Limitations & Program Ceilings (The "Rec Ceiling")
* **The 2-Hour Weekly Mat Cap:** A child training at DSAL receives only 1 to 2 hours of mat time per week. In wrestling, 2 hours per week is insufficient to develop muscle memory, chain-wrestling reflexes, or high-level cardiovascular endurance.
* **No Scholastic Travel Circuit:** DSAL does not field a competitive travel team for SCYWAY regional tournaments, USA Wrestling State Championships, or national events.
* **Zero Adult, Sambo, or Jiu-Jitsu Training:** DSAL's wrestling program stops strictly at high school graduation. Adults, college grapplers, and BJJ practitioners have zero training access.

1. **Establish the Formal "Next-Step Academy" Partnership:**
   * Head Coach **Naweed Zemaryalai** and Founder **Karim Zemaryalai** should reach out to Yero Washington and Coach Juana Juarez. 
   * UWC positions itself not as a rival, but as the **elite training partner** for DSAL athletes who outgrow beginner rec drills and want to compete in high school and college.
   * UWC can sponsor **2 to 4 full "Junior Olympian Need-Based Scholarships"** per semester for standout DSAL student-athletes, removing the financial barrier.
2. **Host Bi-Monthly Free Clinics at DSAL:**
   * Coach Naweed (Chabot College Coach) and Olympian Karim Zemaryalai can volunteer a Saturday morning masterclass at 360 Laurel Ave. 
   * Hand every participant a co-branded flyer: *"Train Year-Round at United Wrestling Club — Hayward’s Olympic Training Facility"*.
3. **Earn `.org` and Municipal Inbound Backlinks:**
   * Secure a permanent partner link on `btsbayarea.org/partners` and `acdsal.org/community-partners`:  
     *Anchor Text:* `United Wrestling Club — Year-Round Elite Wrestling Academy (Hayward, CA)`.
4. **Publish a High-Converting Local Bridge Page on `unitedwrestlingclub.com`:**

### Profile 2: San Francisco Police Activities League — SFPAL Wrestling

* **Domain:** [sfpal.org](https://www.sfpal.org/) | [sfpal.org/programs/wrestling-camp](https://www.sfpal.org/programs/wrestling-camp/)
* **Physical Location:** **San Francisco Police Academy Gymnasium**, 350 Amber Drive, San Francisco, CA 94131
* **Distance to UWC:** ~26 miles (via CA-92 W / I-280 N across San Mateo Bridge)
* **OpenSEO Footprint:** `sfpal.org`: **541 monthly organic visits | 260 ranked keywords**
* **SERP Ranks:** **#7 and #12 Organic** for *"youth wrestling clubs bay area"*
* **Tax & Governance Model:** 501(c)(3) Police Activities League non-profit established in 1959 in cooperation with the San Francisco Police Department (SFPD).

#### A. Organizational Mission, Funding & Target Demographic
SFPAL is an established San Francisco civic institution dedicated to creating positive relationships between police officers and city youth through sports. SFPAL offers low-cost and free athletic programs across baseball, basketball, cheer, flag football, martial arts/jiu-jitsu, and wrestling. The program is supported by police officer volunteers, municipal grants, and private corporate philanthropy.

#### B. Current Program Offerings, Facilities & Weekly Schedule
* **Program:** SFPAL Youth Wrestling Camp.
* **Venue:** SF Police Academy Gym, 350 Amber Drive (Diamond Heights / Glen Park area of San Francisco).
* **Schedule:** **Strictly Summer Only (June through July)**. Practices held **Mondays and Thursdays from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM** (4 hours/week for 6–8 weeks).
* **Target Audience:** Co-ed youth ages 12–17 (middle and high school students).
* **Cost:** Free or nominal registration fee (~$0–$50 with complete fee waivers upon request).

#### C. Coaching Staff & Institutional Ties
* Instructed by volunteer SFPD officers with collegiate/high school wrestling backgrounds, supported by guest local high school coaches (e.g., Riordan, Washington, Galileo).
* Backlinked and promoted on `sfgov.org`, SFPD station newsletters, and local San Francisco unified school district (SFUSD) athletic portals.

#### D. Algorithmic Root Cause: Why SFPAL Outranks Commercial Gyms
`sfpal.org` has operated continuously for decades with high civic trust:
1. **Domain Age and Institutional Backlinks:** Inbound links from city government pages (`sfgov.org`), official police portals, and media outlets give the domain high PageRank.
2. **Dedicated Program Page Architecture:** Unlike many municipal sites that bury wrestling in a PDF flyer, SFPAL maintains a dedicated static URL (`/programs/wrestling-camp/`) with clean H1 tags, age parameters, and location details that Google ranks for *"youth wrestling clubs bay area"*.

#### E. Operational Limitations & Program Ceilings
* **The 10-Month Void:** SFPAL shuts down wrestling completely from August through May. For 10 months of the year, SFPAL wrestlers have **zero practices**.
* **Summer Rec Limit:** It is structured as an introductory camp rather than a year-round competitive club. There is no tournament coaching or off-season weight management.
* **Age Exclusions:** Does not cater to youth under age 12 (elementary/Pre-K) or adults/collegiate athletes.

#### F. Strategic Action Plan for United Wrestling Club
1. **Target the Post-Camp Transition in July/August:**
   * Publish a regional guide: `/compare/sf-peninsula-youth-wrestling`.
   * Title: *"Where to Wrestle in the Bay Area After Summer Camp | Year-Round High School & Youth Wrestling"*
   * Explicitly highlight: *"Completed SFPAL, Belmont, or local summer wrestling camps and looking for year-round training? United Wrestling Club provides 6 days a week of Olympic Folkstyle and Freestyle wrestling just across the San Mateo Bridge."*
2. **Offer East Bay / Peninsula Carpool Connections:**
   * Many families in San Francisco and the northern Peninsula (San Mateo, Foster City) commute across CA-92 to the East Bay. Highlight UWC's easy access off I-880 / CA-92 in Hayward.

### Profile 3: San Francisco Elite Wrestling & SF Beach Wrestling

* **Domains:** [sfelite.org](http://sfelite.org/) | `sfelitewrestling.org` | `sfbeachwrestling.org`
* **Physical Location:** Archbishop Riordan High School (175 Phelan Ave / Frida Kahlo Way, SF) & Ocean Beach, San Francisco, CA
* **Distance to UWC:** ~27 miles (across San Mateo Bridge / I-280)
* **OpenSEO Footprint:** ~**45 monthly organic visits | 32 keywords** across legacy domains; high ranking through USA Wrestling club directories.
* **SERP Ranks:** **#4, #5 & #7 Organic** for *"youth wrestling clubs bay area"* and *"wrestling clubs alameda county"*
* **Tax & Governance Model:** 501(c)(3) Amateur Athletic Non-Profit (founded 1988; incorporated 1994), run entirely by volunteer board members and coaches.

#### A. Organizational Mission, Funding & Target Demographic
San Francisco Elite Wrestling (SF Elite) was founded in **1988** by **Vittorio ("Vic") Anastasio**, a recognized figure in San Francisco wrestling who became head coach at Archbishop Riordan High School in 1984 at age 20. Originally affiliated with the California JETS (Junior Elite Training Squads), SF Elite was incorporated as a 501(c)(3) non-profit in 1994. Its mission is to support San Francisco amateur youth, middle school, and high school wrestlers competing in USA Wrestling, CIF, and local regional leagues (Peninsula Wrestling League).

In addition, members of this network run **SF Beach Wrestling**, an informal, free USA Wrestling-chartered club that organizes sand grappling and beach wrestling practices at Ocean Beach during the summer.

#### B. Current Program Offerings, Facilities & Weekly Schedule
* **Venues:** Operates inside high school wrestling rooms (primarily Archbishop Riordan High School wrestling annex) and outdoor sessions at Ocean Beach.
* **Schedule:** Highly variable; typically runs 2–3 evenings per week during spring/summer freestyle season (March–July) and off-season open mats.
* **Cost:** Low-cost seasonal club fees (~$150–$250/season) plus USA Wrestling card.

#### C. Coaching Staff & Institutional Ties
* Founded by Vic Anastasio; staffed by volunteer Riordan and SF high school alumni.
* Deep historical ties to the CIF San Francisco Section, the Peninsula Wrestling League, and local Catholic school athletic programs.

#### D. Algorithmic Root Cause: Why They Outrank Modern Gyms
* **38-Year Historic Domain & Organizational Footprint:** Active since 1988, SF Elite was one of the earliest amateur wrestling clubs indexed on the web in Northern California.
* **USA Wrestling Directory Inclusion:** Direct club directory listings on `usawmembership.com` and `themat.com` pass continuous topical equity.
* **Keyword Saturation in Historical Forums:** Mentioned in hundreds of high school wrestling forum threads on *The California Wrestler* (`thecaliforniawrestler.com`), *CalGrappler*, and Bay Area sports archives.

#### E. Operational Limitations & Program Ceilings
* **Lack of Dedicated Commercial Facility:** Dependent on high school administrative approval and room availability. Subject to cancellations during school exams, athletic conflicts, or campus closures.
* **Volunteer-Only Staffing:** Practices are subject to volunteer coach availability rather than full-time professional coaching staff.
* **No Multi-Discipline Integration:** Zero BJJ, zero Combat Sambo, zero striking, zero adult novice programs.

#### F. Strategic Action Plan for United Wrestling Club
1. **Contrast Dedicated Facility vs. High School Auxiliary Room:**
   * On UWC's `/programs/competition-team` page, emphasize: *"Dedicated 6-Day/Week Private Combat Mat Room — No school district room cancellations, no sharing space with cheer or basketball. Full-time international coaching staff."*
2. **Host an Annual "Bay Area Non-Profit Invitational Open Mat":**
   * UWC can invite SF Elite, Paragon, and DSAL wrestlers to a free, highly structured Saturday regional scrimmage in Hayward. This establishes UWC as the premier central hub of Northern California wrestling.
3. **Capture Beach & Freestyle Search Traffic:**
   * Publish a guide on UWC's site: `/bay-area-freestyle-and-greco-roman-wrestling`. Position UWC's Olympic freestyle curriculum under 1980 Olympian Karim Zemaryalai as the premier regional development program.

### Profile 4: Paragon Wrestling Club

* **Domain:** [paragonwrestlingclub.com](http://www.paragonwrestlingclub.com/)
* **Physical Location:** **City College of San Francisco (CCSF)**, 175 Frida Kahlo Way, San Francisco, CA 94112 / Sacred Heart Cathedral Prep / 1055 Ellis St
* **Distance to UWC:** ~25 miles (via CA-92 W / I-280 N)
* **OpenSEO Footprint:** ~**30 monthly organic visits | 22 keywords**; strong regional competitor SERP overlap (#16 in Bay Area).
* **SERP Ranks:** **#10 Maps** in Tri-City zoom-out; **#18 Organic** for *"wrestling clubs alameda county"*
* **Tax & Governance Model:** 501(c)(3) Youth Athletic Non-Profit (IRS Tax-Exempt Determination: March 2017).

#### A. Organizational Mission, Funding & Target Demographic
Paragon Wrestling Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization established to advance youth and scholastic wrestling in San Francisco and the northern Peninsula. The club is directed by **James Woods**, a UC Berkeley alumnus who joined the club in 2016, took over as Director in 2019, and concurrently serves as the Head Wrestling Coach at Sacred Heart Cathedral Preparatory (SHCP).

Paragon acts as a feeder and off-season development room for San Francisco private and public school wrestlers, particularly student-athletes aiming for West Catholic Athletic League (WCAL) and CIF Central Coast Section (CCS) competition.

#### B. Current Program Offerings, Facilities & Weekly Schedule
* **Venues:** Operates primarily out of City College of San Francisco (CCSF) athletic wrestling room and Sacred Heart Cathedral facilities.
* **Schedule:** Seasonal practice schedules running 2 to 3 evenings per week (typically Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays) tailored to scholastic off-season cycles (Spring Freestyle/Greco and Fall Pre-Season).
* **Cost:** Seasonal non-profit dues (~$200–$350 per seasonal block) with financial aid available via direct application.

#### C. Coaching Staff & Institutional Ties
* **Club Director / Head Coach:** **James Woods** (UC Berkeley; Head Coach at Sacred Heart Cathedral Prep).
* Supported by collegiate guest clinicians from Stanford, SF State, and Cal.
* Strong pipeline into the West Catholic Athletic League (Riordan, Sacred Heart, St. Ignatius, Bellarmine).

#### D. Algorithmic Root Cause: Why Paragon Shows Up in Regional SERPs
* **Academic Institutional Association (`.edu` Signals):** By hosting practices at City College of San Francisco (`ccsf.edu`) and Sacred Heart Cathedral (`shcathletics.com`), Paragon inherits co-citation authority with established San Francisco educational domains.
* **Active 501(c)(3) Verification:** Verified non-profit profiles on PayPal Giving Fund, Zeffy, and ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer provide high-trust entity validation to search engines.

#### E. Operational Limitations & Program Ceilings
* **Commute Friction for East Bay Athletes:** Located across the Bay; not practical for weekday training for families living in Hayward, Fremont, or Union City.
* **Scholastic Prep Focus Only:** Tailored almost exclusively toward competitive high school and middle school wrestlers. No adult beginner wrestling, no martial arts cross-training, no morning or midday classes.

#### F. Strategic Action Plan for United Wrestling Club
1. **The Collegiate Alumni Connection (Chabot / SF State / Cal):**
   * Coach **Naweed Zemaryalai** wrestled at Chabot College and San Francisco State University (B.S. in Kinesiology) and currently coaches at Chabot. James Woods is a Cal graduate.
   * Naweed can establish a friendly collegiate-coaching relationship with Woods, positioning UWC as the premier East Bay off-season training facility for athletes who cannot travel into SF on weeknights.
2. **East Bay Geo-Targeting:**
   * Paragon ranks #18 Organic in Alameda County simply because Alameda County has so few dedicated wrestling clubs. By publishing `/wrestling-clubs-alameda-county` and `/east-bay-scholastic-wrestling-camp`, UWC will immediately surpass Paragon across all East Bay SERPs.

#### A. Organizational Mission, Funding & Target Demographic
* **Petaluma Wrestling Club (PWC):** Founded to serve Sonoma and North Bay youth wrestlers, PWC operates seasonal folkstyle, freestyle, and youth clinics out of junior high facilities. Like UWC, Petaluma WC uses **WrestlingIQ** for registration and roster management.
* **Lamorinda Wrestling Club:** Serves the affluent Contra Costa County communities of **Lafayette, Moraga, and Orinda (Lamorinda)**. Structured as a recreational youth development league under the Orinda Youth Association, it introduces suburban elementary and middle school kids (ages 7–14) to wrestling.

#### B. Current Program Offerings, Facilities & Weekly Schedule
* **Petaluma WC:** Runs 4 seasonal blocks (Fall pre-season, Winter scholastic, Spring freestyle, Summer camp) utilizing school multi-purpose rooms.
* **Lamorinda WC:** **Strictly seasonal for 12 weeks only (November 1 through January 31)**. Registration opens in August and closes in October. Once January ends, the club ceases all operations until the following autumn. Practices are held 2 evenings per week.
* **Cost:** ~$250–$350 per season.

#### C. Coaching Staff & Institutional Ties
* Lamorinda is staffed by collegiate alumni (including Lehigh University wrestler Michael Deeming) teaching foundational curriculums (Cary Kolat drill systems).
* Tied closely to Miramonte High School and Campolindo High School booster programs.

#### D. Algorithmic Root Cause: Why They Rank Across Bay Area SERPs
* **Clean Community Association & Exact Keyword Matching:** Petaluma WC has clean metadata targeting *"youth wrestling club bay area"*, and Lamorinda targets *"wrestling club east bay"*.
* **Civic Recreation Portals:** Both clubs are listed in city community center recreation guides (e.g., Orinda Parks & Rec, ActivityHero, Enrichment Kids), creating persistent local citation signals.

#### E. Operational Limitations & Program Ceilings
* **The 40-Week Off-Season (Lamorinda):** Lamorinda shuts down for 9 to 10 months every year. A wrestler living in Orinda or Lafayette who wants to become a CIF state qualifier cannot train in Lamorinda between February and October.
* **Geographic Commute (Petaluma):** Petaluma is over 55 miles north of the East Bay.

#### F. Strategic Action Plan for United Wrestling Club
1. **Capture Lamorinda's Off-Season Athletes (February – October):**
   * Families in Lafayette, Moraga, and Walnut Creek are accustomed to driving 20–25 minutes down CA-24 / I-680 / I-580 for elite youth coaching.
   * Deploy a dedicated regional landing page: `/east-bay-off-season-wrestling-camp`.
   * Target queries: *"off season wrestling clubs east bay"*, *"year round youth wrestling contra costa"*, *"wrestling training after middle school season"*.
2. **Benchmark WrestlingIQ Implementation:**
   * Both Petaluma WC and UWC utilize **WrestlingIQ** for billing. UWC should leverage WrestlingIQ’s public calendar integration and direct registration links on its site to provide a seamless registration experience superior to non-profit waitlists.
