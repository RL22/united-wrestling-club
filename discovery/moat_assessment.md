# Moat assessment: is lineage UWC's moat?

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Question:** Is the 1980 Olympic lineage a moat? What are UWC's top three?
- **Standard:** A moat must be (1) hard for a competitor to copy, (2) valued by the buyer's purchase decision, and (3) provable on the site today.

## Verdict

**No. Lineage is a differentiator, not a moat.** It passes test 1 (no competitor can claim it), but it fails tests 2 and 3:

- **Test 2, buyer value:** the main buyer is a parent of a 5-to-13-year-old. PRODUCT.md lists their concerns as character, safety, and attention from coaches. "Olympian founder" does not answer any of those in five seconds. It matters more to adult grapplers and scholastic athletes.
- **Test 3, provable:** Karim boycotted the 1980 Moscow Games (client, Nov 5, 2026). So "Moscow Olympian" is not accurate, and "qualifier" needs confirmation of what he qualified for. The claim also has no source in the project. The discovery docs say "1980 Moscow Olympics qualifier", while others say "Olympian". The US boycotted the 1980 Moscow Games, so any 1980 claim needs exact wording, a source, and Karim's confirmation. The fact-check list already asks for this (`todos.md` P1, and `discovery/uwc_marketing_plan.md` line 704).
- **Fades:** a 1980 result is fixed. The living coaches and results are what compound.

Keep the lineage as a proof point, with verified wording. Stop calling it the moat in strategy and copy.

## Recommended top three

1. **Dedicated wrestling room, year-round, for scholastic and adult wrestlers** (East Bay corridor).
2. **Named coaches with capped ratios** (verified ratio and credentials).
3. **Transparent, no-contract pricing with a low-risk trial** (acquisition advantage).

## What has to be true for these to hold

- **Moat 1:** a list of CIF, NCS, SCYWAY, and state results with names (with permission), and written feeder relationships with Hayward, James Logan, and Fremont schools. Neither is in the project yet.
- **Moat 2:** a verified coach roster with credentials and a written ratio policy. Both are on the fact-check list.
- **Moat 3:** a retention number. Without it, "no contract" is only a price claim. Track 3-month and 12-month retention from the WrestlingIQ roster.
