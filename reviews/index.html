Voices from the Mat

Aijaz Patel ★★★★★ Google Review

“I brought my 8-year-old son to UWC and honestly, we couldn’t be happier. From the moment we walked in, Nawid, Tariq, and Umaid welcomed us with open arms. They didn’t rush the process—they brought my son in slowly, helped him ease into the sport, and most importantly, made sure he felt safe and seen. I’ve watched my son grow more confident, disciplined, and mentally stronger every week... And for the parents? You feel the hospitality the second you walk in, whether it’s being offered tea or just having a friendly conversation while watching practice. It really feels like a community. Highly recommend UWC to any parent looking for a place where their child can grow—not just in sport, but in life.”

David Johnson ★★★★★ Google Review

“The best regardless of age—come here to get better. Celebrity athletes train here alongside everyday grinders, with elite instruction in no-gi, gi, boxing, and wrestling (folkstyle, freestyle, and Greco). The coaching staff and ownership team are incredibly accomplished—knowledgeable, invested, and committed to athlete development at every level. Healthy competition, humble egos, and a room full of experienced grapplers and MMA fighters pushing each other daily. Hell of a gym — come get some work in 🥊🤼🥋”

Ingoo Kwak ★★★★★ Google Review

“We were visiting the Bay Area from out of state for a few days and were looking for a youth wrestling club where my son could get some extra practice and experience a different training environment. We found United Wrestling Club, and it turned out to be an incredible experience. We didn’t expect much individual attention, but the coaches took the time to work with him closely — just like every other student. Everyone was welcoming, supportive, and treated him like part of the team from the very start. To our surprise, he was even invited to join the team for a local tournament, which was an amazing experience. If we visit the Bay Area again, we will definitely be coming back.”
