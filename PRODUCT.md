## 1. Product Purpose & Value Proposition
United Wrestling Club is Northern California's premier Olympic and Folkstyle wrestling academy. Founded by Dagestani tournament medalist Karim Zemaryalai (1980 Olympic wording pending confirmation) and led by Head Coach Naweed Zemaryalai (SFSU Kinesiology, Chabot College Coach), UWC trains youth competitors (ages 5–18) and adults in collegiate wrestling, Wrestle-Jitsu, Combat Sambo, and athletic conditioning.

## 2. Target Audience & Users
1. **Parents of Youth Athletes (Ages 5–13):** Seeking character development, physical discipline, resilience, anti-bullying confidence, and high coach-to-student attention in a safe, clean environment.
2. **Scholastic & High School Wrestlers (Ages 14–18):** Seeking CIF state qualification, NCAA recruitment prep, advanced hand-fighting, and live tournament-pace competition.
3. **Adult Grapplers & Combat Athletes (Ages 18+):** BJJ practitioners, judoka, and hobbyists wanting elite takedown technique, cage control, and high-intensity conditioning without egos.
4. **Local East Bay Families:** Residents across Hayward, Union City, Fremont, Newark, Castro Valley, and San Leandro looking for local athletic excellence.

## 3. Brand Personality & Tone
- **Tone:** Authoritative, disciplined, authentic, uncompromising, community-grounded.
- **Visual Feel:** Heavyweight mat room energy, Olympic lineage, raw cinematic contrast, calibrated crimson (`#902524`), warm charcoal surfaces, clean sans-serif typography (`Montserrat`), tack-sharp athletic photography.
- **Anti-References:** 
  - Sterile corporate fitness franchises (Planet Fitness, Equinox).
  - McDojo cartoonish karate / strip-mall belt-factories.
  - Vague AI-generated fitness imagery with zero local authenticity.
  - Hidden pricing traps, 12-month lock-in contracts, and multi-step registration friction.

## 4. Strategic Principles for Homepage Redesign
1. **Proof over pedigree:** Lead with what a parent can check: a dedicated wrestling room in the East Bay with year-round scholastic and adult classes, named coaches with verified credentials and a capped ratio, and a transparent no-contract price with a $15 trial. Lineage is a supporting proof point, shown only in wording the founder has confirmed. It does not lead the page. Source: `discovery/moat_assessment.md` (Nov 5, 2026).
2. **Local Physical Grounding:** Prominently display the physical Hayward facility address, USA Wrestling RTC sanctioning (#513), and 42+ 5-star local parent reviews.
3. **High-Converting 1-Click Trial Capture:** Replace external WrestlingIQ redirects with a frictionless native 1-click modal for the \$15 3-Day Mat Pass or Free Intro Session.
4. **Structured Program Silos:** Explicitly route users into Youth Academy (5–13), Varsity Prep (14–18), Adult Wrestle-Jitsu (18+), and Saturday Open Mat.
5. **Radical Price Transparency:** Flat \$139.99/mo with zero contracts and zero cancellation fees.
