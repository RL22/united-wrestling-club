PARENT PEACE OF MIND

SAFETY FIRST.
TECHNIQUE ALWAYS.

Combat sports require trust. As a USA Wrestling sanctioned RTC, our room operates with strict hygiene, medical protocol, and structured coaching.

MAT ROOM SAFETY STANDARDS

Antimicrobial Sanitization: 3,000 sq ft Dollamur mats mopped with hospital-grade fungicidal & virucidal wash twice daily.
1:6 Coach Ratio: Every youth athlete is watched and guided by a coach on the mat, not left unsupervised.
Zero-Ego Room Culture: Respect for training partners is mandatory. Arrogance or reckless aggression results in immediate dismissal.
My child has zero experience. Will they get hurt or overwhelmed? +
Every beginner starts with basic body mechanics: stance, footwork, balance, and learning how to fall safely (breakfalls and rolls). We match practice partners strictly by size, weight, and experience level. Beginners are never thrown into aggressive live matches on their first days.
How do you keep the mats sanitized and prevent skin infections? +
Our 3,000 sq ft seamless Dollamur mats are sanitized before and after every training block using hospital-grade antimicrobial solutions (effective against MRSA, ringworm, and staph). Athletes must wear clean gear, and street shoes are strictly forbidden on the wrestling surface.
What gear do we need to bring for the $15 3-Day Mat Pass? +
Just wear comfortable athletic shorts (no zippers, pockets, or metal hardware) and a clean t-shirt or rashguard, plus a water bottle. If you already own wrestling shoes, bring them; otherwise, clean socks or bare feet on the mat are fine for your first practice while we perform your sizing fitting.
Are there contracts, cancellation fees, or unexpected costs? +
None. We believe in radical pricing transparency. The 3-Day Mat Pass is a one-time $15 charge. Monthly memberships are $139.99/mo billed month-to-month with zero registration fees. You can pause or cancel at any time directly through WrestlingIQ.
Can adult beginners without high school wrestling experience join? +
Yes! Our Adult & Wrestle-Jitsu class is engineered specifically for adults (18+) who either train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and want real takedown mastery, or simply want the incredible conditioning and functional strength that wrestling builds without having their ego bruised.
