For the parent in your corner

Big questions.
Honest answers.

A new sport can feel like a big step. We’ll help you and your athlete take the first one.

Ask Coach Naweed a question ↗
My child has never wrestled. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Beginners learn stance, movement and basic technique before progressing. Your child joins an age-appropriate group, and the coach helps match training partners by size and experience. The Mat Pass is a chance to get comfortable, not a tryout.

Is wrestling safe? What about injuries?

Wrestling is a contact sport, so risk can’t be eliminated. We teach controlled technique and build intensity as athletes develop. Coaches supervise practice and emphasize suitable partners, communication and respect. Tell Coach Naweed about any injury or limitation before training.

What if my child is shy or nervous?

That’s a normal first-day feeling. Let the coach know before you arrive. We focus on small wins, clear instruction and learning to be a good partner. You don’t need a competitive personality to start building confidence here.

What should we bring to the first practice?

Bring water and clean, comfortable athletic clothes without zippers or jewelry. The Mat Pass includes gear fitting. Text us about footwear before your visit, and we’ll help you get ready without buying a full kit first.

How do you keep the mats clean?

Our room has 3,000 square feet of seamless Dollamur mats with hospital-grade antimicrobial sanitization. Clean training clothes, proper mat footwear and good personal hygiene are part of every athlete’s responsibility. Don’t train with an illness or suspected skin infection; contact the coach first.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

No. Unlimited training is $139.99 per month with no contracts or initiation fees. Try the $15 3-Day Mat Pass first. If it feels like your room, talk to us about membership and the 15% family / sibling discount.

Can adults start with no wrestling experience?

Yes. Adult beginners and BJJ practitioners train here, too. Tell the coach your experience and goals so you can start with appropriate technique and partners. You don’t have to get in shape before showing up.
